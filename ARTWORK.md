# Hibi artwork and font credits

All sticker images in `public/stickers/` are derived from freely licensed photographs on Wikimedia Commons. They were downloaded once while building the demo; the app makes no runtime requests.

**Processing:** the subject was cut out locally with [rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) (`isnet-general-use` model), small stray fragments were removed, a white die-cut border was added, and the result was resized to at most 520px, and saved as WebP. For flat paper items (the ticket and postcard), the whole photo was kept and only the border was added. The derived stickers are shared under the same license as their source.

These are demo assets only. Wording on the source objects (labels, ticket text, and so on) belongs to the original photos and does not imply any endorsement.
